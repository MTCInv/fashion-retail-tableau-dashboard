# Rationale (fashion retail, 10 brands, latest FY2024/25)
**Metrics and data:** We picked four metrics. Revenue (USD bn) is the standard measure of scale and market share. Operating margin (%) shows how efficiently a brand turns sales into profit, reflecting its pricing power and supply-chain speed. Year-on-year revenue growth (%) shows momentum, separating today's leader from tomorrow's challenger. Store count shows physical reach, still the main sales channel in fashion. Together they cover scale, profitability, momentum and reach. All figures come from the companies' own audited FY2024/25 results (Inditex, H&M, Fast Retailing, ABF, Next, Mango, Gap Inc, ASOS, URBN), the most reliable and comparable public source. They are converted to USD at ECB 2024 average rates. Where a brand is a segment of a group, we use segment or concept revenue and growth. If no brand-level profit is published, the parent-group margin is used and flagged (Zara, Bershka, Gap, Urban Outfitters). Mango's margin is EBITDA and Next's is profit before tax. Brand Finance Apparel 50 2025 (Zara brand value $18.1bn, ranked 7th; Uniqlo $10.1bn, 11th) supports the brand-strength view.

## Key observations
1. Zara is the largest brand by revenue (~$30.1bn), ahead of H&M (~$22.2bn) and Uniqlo (~$17.5bn).
2. Uniqlo grew fastest (+13.6%) at a 16.6% margin. Bershka (+11.8%) and Next (+8.2%) also grew strongly.
3. H&M has the most stores (4,253) but a 7.4% margin and shrinking sales in SEK (-0.7%).
4. ASOS is the only loss-making brand (-2.8% adjusted EBIT margin) with like-for-like sales down 16%.
5. Urban Outfitters (-7.7%) and Gap (flat) are the smallest and stagnant.

## Conclusions
1. **Zara (Inditex) is the market leader**: it has the highest revenue and the highest margin (19.6%, group level), and it ranks top among these brands in Brand Finance.
2. Fast, demand-led supply chains (Inditex, Uniqlo) beat sheer store count (H&M) for profit.
3. Online-only models struggled; omnichannel brands are winning.
4. Uniqlo is the main challenger on growth and margin.
